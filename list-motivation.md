# Motivation Post Ideas

One block per post. `- [ ]` = todo, `- [x]` = built.
The `make-motivation` skill takes the first unchecked block and ticks it off once
the files land in `out/motivation/day-NN/`.

## Block format

```
- [ ] **Day N — carousel — Title**
      idea: what the post argues, one or two sentences
      angle: why this isn't the version everyone has already scrolled past
      color: lavender | royal-blue | slate-navy | warm-beige | mustard | red
```

- **`type` lives in the title** (`carousel` or `reel`), so a day can hold any mix —
  two carousels, one of each, three reels.
- **`idea`** is the claim. Specific enough that someone could disagree with it;
  "believe in yourself" is a mug, not a post.
- **`angle`** is what makes it yours. Usually the mechanism — _why_ the thing is
  true, not just that it is. This field decides whether the post lands.
- **`color`** is optional. Leave it out and the skill picks one the last few posts
  didn't use. One flat colour per post, never mixed inside a carousel.
- **Carousel or reel?** Needs a second sentence to land → carousel (4–8 slides of
  one continuous thought). Lands in six words → reel (one line, ~11s).

Only the `- [ ]` line is parsed. Indentation on the other lines is cosmetic.
